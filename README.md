Overview

This project simulates a compliance-focused invoicing platform that enables businesses create, validate, and submit invoices to a government tax authority system.

The prototype was created as part of a GovTech-focused product case study to demonstrate:

Product thinking
Technical product management capability
AI-assisted rapid prototyping
Compliance workflow design
Validation and error-handling systems
User-centered enterprise workflows

The system simulates how a platform like Remita could support businesses in complying with government e‑invoicing requirements.

Problem Statement

As regulations increasingly adopt digital tax compliance systems, businesses face challenges with:

Manual invoice submission processes
Complex compliance requirements
Validation failures and rejected invoices
Poor visibility into submission status
Difficulty handling structured tax data

This prototype explores how a product can reduce compliance friction by introducing upstream validation, structured workflows, and guided error handling before invoice transmission.

Product Thinking & Trade-Offs

A key focus of this project was balancing compliance requirements with usability.

Major Product Trade-Offs
Decision	Trade-Off
Strict validation rules	Reduced user flexibility
Real-time validation	Slightly slower workflow
Compliance-first architecture	Increased product complexity
Structured onboarding	More setup effort for new users

The product prioritizes submission reliability and compliance integrity over speed or minimal user input.
